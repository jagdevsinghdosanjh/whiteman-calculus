# Derivatives – Theory Overview

## 1. Concept

The derivative of a function \(f(x)\) at a point \(x_0\) measures the instantaneous rate of change.

## 2. Formal Definition



\[
f'(x_0) = \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{f(x_0 + h) - f(x_0)}{h}
\]



## 3. Geometric Interpretation

The derivative is the slope of the tangent line to the graph of \(f(x)\) at \(x_0\).
